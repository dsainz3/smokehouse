# Cold Smoking Overview

## Context

- Smoker / Grill: Pit Boss Austin XL (with cold smoke attachment or external smoke generator)
- Cook Style: Cold Smoke
- Target temp: **Below 90°F** — ideally 68–86°F
- Notes: Cold smoking on a pellet grill requires an external cold smoke generator (e.g. A-MAZE-N
  pellet tube/tray). The Pit Boss Austin XL does not cold smoke natively — the auger fires at
  minimum ~150–160°F.

## How to Cold Smoke on the Pit Boss Austin XL

1. **Do not light the Pit Boss.** The grill provides the chamber only.
2. Light the A-MAZE-N tube or tray with a torch; let it establish a smolder (not a flame).
3. Place the lit tube/tray at one end of the grill grates.
4. Place food on the grates away from the smoke source.
5. Close the lid; open the top vent slightly to allow airflow and smoke movement.
6. Monitor ambient temp with a grill-level thermometer — if the chamber creeps above 90°F,
   prop the lid slightly or move to a cooler ambient environment.
7. Cold smoke is best done on cool days (under 60°F ambient) or at night.

## Common Cold-Smoked Foods

| Food | Cure required? | Smoke duration | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cheese | No | 2–4 hours | Wrap and rest in fridge 24–48 hrs after for smoke to mellow |
| Salmon (lox) | Yes (salt + sugar cure) | 4–8 hours | Cure 12–24 hrs first; eat cold |
| Bacon (pre-cure) | Yes (cure + equilibrium cure) | 4–6 hours | Cook to 145°F before eating |
| Salt / finishing salt | No | 2–4 hours | Spread thin on a tray |
| Butter | No | 1–2 hours | Unwrapped block; absorbs smoke quickly |
| Nuts | No | 2–3 hours | Spread in a single layer on a tray |

---

## Food Safety Summary

- **Cure proteins** (fish, pork) before cold smoking. Use proper curing ratios — do not guess.
- Keep chamber **below 90°F** for the entire smoke. Above that, the danger zone activates
  without the food reaching a safe internal temp.
- Refrigerate immediately after cold smoking; do not leave at room temp.
- When in doubt, refer to USDA or a trusted curing resource before attempting proteins.
