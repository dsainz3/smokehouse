# Pit Boss Austin XL Notes

Primary smoker used for all recipes in this repository.

## Heat Characteristics

- Right side runs hotter than left.

### Placement Strategy

- Thick cuts → right side
- Thin cuts → left side
- Poultry: legs/thighs toward the right side

## General Advice

- Verify doneness with internal temps.
- Cook to temperature, not time.

## Recommended Accessories

- [BBQ Resting Blanket (21")](bbq_resting_blanket.md) — insulated wrap for the rest phase
- [12" Pellet Smoker Tube](pellet_smoker_tube.md) — required for cold smoking; adds extra
  smoke on hot cooks too
