# 12" Pellet Smoker Tube (Cold/Hot Smoke Generator)

A 12" stainless steel tube that burns compressed wood pellets to produce smoke independently
of the grill's heat source. Essential for cold smoking on the Pit Boss Austin XL.

- [Amazon — 12" Pellet Smoker Tube](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07JGVBKN3)

The Pit Boss Austin XL can't cold smoke on its own — the auger fires at a minimum of ~150–160°F,
which is too hot. This tube solves that. I light it, set it in the grill with the power off,
and it produces clean smoke for 2–5 hours with zero heat from the grill itself.

I also use it during hot smokes and grilling sessions to layer in additional or complementary
smoke on top of what the Pit Boss pellets already produce. For example, if the grill is loaded
with hickory pellets but I want a touch of cherry or apple alongside it, I'll run the tube with
a different wood to blend the profiles without swapping out the hopper. It's also useful when
grilling at higher temps where the Pit Boss burns pellets fast and clean (less smoke) — the tube
keeps steady smoke going throughout the cook.

## Use Case

- **Cold smoking:** primary smoke source when the Pit Boss is off (cheese, salmon, butter,
  salt, nuts) — see [Cold Smoking](../cold_smoking/)
- **Hot smoking:** adds extra smoke output on top of what the Pit Boss pellets already produce;
  useful for stronger smoke flavor without cranking temps

## How to Use

1. Fill the tube with wood pellets (don't pack tight — airflow is needed)
2. Light one end with a butane torch; let it establish a solid smolder for 1–2 minutes
3. Blow out the flame — it should smolder, not burn open flame
4. Place in the smoker on the grates, lit end toward airflow
5. For cold smoking: place in the Pit Boss (grill off) with the top vent cracked slightly

## Notes

- Burns approximately **2–5 hours** depending on pellet type and airflow
- Works with any wood pellets — match to the recipe (hickory, apple, cherry, etc.)
- Stainless steel — dishwasher safe; clean after each use to prevent off-flavors
- Includes a cleaning brush
- For cold smoking, monitor ambient chamber temp; the tube alone can raise it a few degrees
