# BBQ Resting Blanket (Insulated Meat Blanket)

An insulated blanket designed to hold heat during the resting phase after a long cook.

- [Amazon — BBQ Resting Blanket (21")](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DS28FM36)

After a long brisket or pork shoulder cook, the rest is just as important as the smoke. I use
this instead of the old cooler-and-towel trick — it's purpose-built, easier to deploy when
your hands are full, and holds temp reliably for 1–2 hours without losing heat through a
towel that wasn't quite thick enough.

## Use Case

- Wrap brisket, pork shoulder, or turkey post-cook to hold temp while juices redistribute
- Replaces the "cooler + towel" method with a purpose-built insulated sleeve
- Keeps meat above serving temp for 1–2+ hours without a cambro or oven

## Notes

- 21" size — fits full packer briskets and large pork butts
- Use after double-wrapping in foil; the blanket goes over the foil wrap
- Not a substitute for a proper rest — still rest at least 30–60 minutes minimum
- Works alongside a cooler for maximum heat retention on overnight holds
